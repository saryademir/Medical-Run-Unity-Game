## 🎮 Overview

**Medical Run** is an adventure/action game built in Unity, where the player controls a child escaping from viruses inside a maze while collecting pills to survive.  
The game is designed in a **top-down** perspective and offers three difficulty levels: *Easy*, *Medium*, and *Hard*.

**Core Goal:** Escape the maze before time runs out, avoid viruses, and collect the most efficient pills to stay alive.

---

## 🧩 Game Concept

- **Genre:** Adventure / Action  
- **Perspective:** Top-down  
- **Mode:** Single Player  
- **Target Audience:** Ages 7–30  
- **Theme:** Health and survival inside a dream world  
- **Core Idea:** A child escapes from viruses inside a dreamlike labyrinth after falling ill, collecting medicine to recover and wake up.

---

## ⚙️ Gameplay Mechanics

- **Viruses:** Three types (Brainkiller, Spotted, One-eye) — each affects health and time differently.  
- **Pills:** Power B, Tutti Frutti, and Magic Calpol — grant immunity, restore health, or add time.  
- **Health Points:** Player starts with 3 HP.  
- **Time:** Each level has a countdown; special pills can extend or reduce time.  
- **Levels:** Three maze levels with increasing complexity.  
- **Pause & End Screens:** Allow pausing, muting, returning to menu, and showing performance stats.
